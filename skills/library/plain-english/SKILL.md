# Plain English for ToggleBank customers

- Lead with the answer in one sentence, then the details.
- Use everyday words: "money back" rather than "remediation", "charge" rather than "debit transaction".
- When you must use a banking term (provisional credit, chargeback), explain it in the same sentence.
- Keep it short: at most five bullets or three short paragraphs.
- End with one clear next step the customer can take.

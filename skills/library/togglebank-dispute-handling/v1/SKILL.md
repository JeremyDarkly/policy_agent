# Handling a disputed transaction

Follow these steps in order.

1. **Identify the transaction.** Confirm the date, amount and merchant from the account profile. If the customer
   hasn't said which transaction, ask. Never ask for a full card number, PIN or password.
2. **Separate fraud from a merchant dispute.**
   - *Unauthorized* (the customer didn't make it): offer to lock the card right away, and say a replacement card
     will follow.
   - *Authorized but wrong* (charged twice, wrong amount, goods not received): suggest contacting the merchant first,
     and say ToggleBank can open a dispute if that doesn't resolve it.
3. **Explain what happens next.** ToggleBank opens a dispute case and investigates.
4. **Don't promise an outcome.** Never say the money will be refunded or that the dispute will succeed. The
   investigation decides.
5. **Hand off.** Opening a dispute needs identity verification, so offer to connect the customer with a banker to
   file it.
